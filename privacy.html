Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shortly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our URL shortening service.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a short link we store:

  • The original long URL you submit
  • A randomly generated short code
  • Creation timestamp and optional expiry time
  • Aggregate click counts

We do not require an account. We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or payment information for the free public service.

Technical data such as browser type, approximate location derived from IP, and referrer may be processed by our hosting and database providers in the ordinary course of serving requests.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and resolve short links
  • To count clicks and display basic statistics
  • To enforce link expiry and delete expired records
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse
  • To improve the service when analytics cookies are enabled

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to remember your cookie preferences and to operate core features. Optional analytics cookies are used only if you consent. See our Cookie Policy for details.

4. Data Storage and Processors

Link data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. Supabase acts as a data processor. Hosting may be provided by GitHub Pages or another static host. These providers process data under their own privacy terms.

5. Retention

Short links are retained until their chosen expiry (12 hours, 24 hours, or forever) or until you request deletion. Expired links are removed from the database. Click counts are aggregate and are not tied to individual visitors beyond the technical logs of the database provider.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Because the free service does not require accounts, most data is not linked to an identifiable person. Contact us if you believe a specific short code contains personal information that should be removed.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository or contact the maintainer listed there.
