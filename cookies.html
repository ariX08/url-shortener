Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Shortly uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make sites work, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential cookies

These are necessary for the service to function. They include:

  • A cookie or local-storage entry that records your cookie consent choices so we do not ask you every time.
  • Any strictly necessary technical cookies required by the hosting platform.

Essential cookies cannot be turned off if you want to use the service.

Analytics cookies (optional)

If you consent, we may use simple analytics to understand overall usage patterns. These are not used for advertising and do not track you across other websites.

3. How to Control Cookies

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Preferences" in the site footer. You can also clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings.

4. Local Storage

We use browser local storage to keep a short history of the links you have created on this device and to store your cookie consent. This data stays on your device.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when our practices change. The date at the top will be revised accordingly.

6. More Information

For broader privacy information see our Privacy Policy.
